Privacy Policy for Abhyasham

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Abhyasham ("we", "us", or "our") is an educational practice application designed to help users practice questions and tests for competitive and government examinations.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Abhyasham, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Phone number: used for phone-number authentication and account access.
  • Profile information: such as your name, username and email address when you choose to provide them.
  • Profile photo: if you choose an image from your device for your profile. The current version may process the selected image locally for display; it is not uploaded to our Firebase Storage.
  • Quiz and test information: answers, scores, correct/incorrect/unanswered counts, test type, selected topics, question identifiers and completion time.
  • Account identifiers: Firebase Authentication may provide a unique user identifier. Guest users may receive an anonymous Firebase identifier.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by the services we use to provide authentication, database and app functionality, such as basic device/app/service information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • provide guest and signed-in app functionality;
  • save profiles and user preferences;
  • record test attempts and calculate scores;
  • display performance statistics and leaderboards;
  • maintain, secure and improve the application; and
  • respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Services We Use

Abhyasham uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support authentication and application data storage. Data processed by these services is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may share or disclose information only as necessary to operate the app, including to service providers such as Google Firebase, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security or integrity of the application and its users.
